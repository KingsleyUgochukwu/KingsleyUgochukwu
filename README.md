Hi there! I'm Kingsley Nnaemeka Ugochukwu

I'm an MSc researcher in Biochemistry and Molecular Biology at Obafemi Awolowo University, Nigeria, with a strong interest in understanding biological systems through the integration of experimental biochemistry, mathematical modelling, and computational approaches.

My research journey began during my undergraduate studies in Biochemistry, where I gained hands-on laboratory experience investigating bioactive compounds, phytochemical properties, and their potential therapeutic applications. This early exposure contributed to my first co-authored scientific publication in the *International Journal of Bipolar Disorders* (2024), exploring the neuroprotective potential of red onion husk extract.

Building on this foundation, my postgraduate research has expanded into enzymology, particularly enzyme purification, kinetics, and biochemical characterization. My growing curiosity about the interactions and regulatory mechanisms underlying complex biological systems has also inspired my transition into Computational Biology, Systems Biology, and Bioinformatics, where I am developing Python-based models to investigate neuronal energy metabolism and ATP dynamics.

I'm interested in interdisciplinary research opportunities, international collaborations, and contributing to reproducible, open-source scientific research.

I'm open to research collaborations, scientific discussions, and PhD opportunities in computational and systems biology.
